FleetTrack Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

FleetTrack Pro is published by Joseph Baldwin. This policy explains how the FleetTrack Pro website, installable PWA, and Capacitor app handle information.

Information stored on your device

Trip records and profile settings are stored in the browser or app's local storage on your device. They may include employee name, admin email address, trip date, odometer readings, calculated distance, destination, trip reason, and client initials. FleetTrack Pro does not operate an account system or send these records to an Axium server.

Location

Location access is requested only when you start live GPS tracking. While tracking, the app uses current coordinates in memory to calculate distance. It does not save the coordinates; a distance and odometer reading may be saved with a trip if you choose to save that trip. You can stop tracking or deny location permission at any time.

Exports and sharing

If you choose Download CSV, the report is created on your device. If you choose Email Report, FleetTrack Pro opens your email app with report details prepared for the admin address you entered. The app does not send the email; you review and send it through your chosen email provider. That provider then handles the information under its own policies.

Hosting and third-party resources

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The app also loads Tailwind CSS from cdn.tailwindcss.com and Phosphor Icons from unpkg.com. Those services and your network provider may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser request metadata, when the app is loaded. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Information in local storage remains on your device until you delete individual trip records, clear the app or browser storage, or remove app data. FleetTrack Pro does not sync records between devices. CSV files and sent emails are managed by you and the services you use to store or send them.

Security

Local storage is protected by the security of your device and browser. Do not use FleetTrack Pro on a shared device unless you are comfortable with its local records. Use your device's passcode and clear app data when appropriate.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. For privacy questions, contact jbaldwin@axiumcyber.com.
